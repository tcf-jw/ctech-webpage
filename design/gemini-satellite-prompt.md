# IMAGE 02 — Satellite Intelligence (Gemini prompt)

Paste everything below the line into Gemini. Matches the global art
direction of the Cellutech image generation pack so it sits in the same
campaign as IMAGE 01–10.

---

You are the Creative Director for Cellutech, a premium enterprise SaaS
platform for AI-powered soil intelligence. You are NOT creating fantasy AI
artwork — you are extending a cohesive commissioned photography library
that looks like it belongs to Apple, Stripe or Planet Labs.

Every image in this campaign is documentary, ultra realistic, Australian,
premium editorial quality: natural lighting, soft atmospheric haze,
cinematic composition, medium contrast, slightly desaturated colours, rich
earth tones.

IMAGE 02

Satellite Intelligence

3840x2160

True nadir (straight-down) satellite perspective, the kind of frame Planet
Labs or Maxar would publish in a press kit.

Australian broadacre farmland from roughly 5–8 km altitude.

A patchwork of irregular paddock shapes in wheat golds, fallow browns and
young-crop greens, stitched together by farm tracks and tree lines.

One meandering creek or river corridor crossing the frame for natural
structure, with darker riparian vegetation tracing its banks.

Soft morning sun, gentle shadow relief revealing paddock texture and
contour lines. A few faint cloud shadows are fine; no clouds obscuring the
land.

Very subtle analysis overlay: thin vector boundary lines traced around a
handful of paddocks, plus a barely-visible sampling grid on one field.
Maximum 15% opacity. Cool cyan tone. The image must still read as real
satellite photography first, software second.

No text.

No logos.

No UI chrome.

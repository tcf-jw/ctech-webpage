# CELLUTECH IMAGE PACK — BATCH 2 (Gemini prompts)

Three additions to the campaign: a microbe macro for the Microbial Biology
pillar, a mining site-monitoring documentary shot, and a green-season
agriculture aerial for palette variety. Run each prompt separately below
the shared preamble. Generate a few candidates of each.

## Shared preamble (paste before every prompt)

You are the Creative Director for Cellutech, a premium enterprise SaaS
platform for AI-powered soil intelligence. You are NOT creating fantasy AI
artwork — you are extending a cohesive commissioned photography library
that looks like it belongs to Apple, Stripe or Planet Labs.

Every image in this campaign is documentary, ultra realistic, Australian,
premium editorial quality: natural lighting, soft atmospheric haze,
cinematic composition, medium contrast, slightly desaturated colours, rich
earth tones. Scientific overlays, if any, stay under 15% opacity.

Negative prompt for all: neon, cyberpunk, cartoon, glowing holograms,
heavy HUD graphics, text labels, oversaturated colours, plastic skin,
fake-looking people, obvious AI artwork.

## IMAGE 11 — Microbial Life

3840x2160

Extreme macro / micrography aesthetic, the kind of frame a university
imaging lab would license to a science publisher.

Living soil biology up close: fine fungal hyphae threading between dark
soil aggregates, translucent root hairs, glistening films of moisture.

Shallow depth of field, one crisp focal band through the frame.

Natural earthy palette — umber, charcoal, bone-white hyphae, a single
restrained note of moss green. Backlit moisture highlights.

It should feel like discovering a hidden city beneath a paddock, not a
sci-fi render. No glowing organisms. No blue laboratory tint.

No text. No logos. No UI chrome.

## IMAGE 12 — Mine Site Monitoring

3840x2160

Real documentary photography, Sony A1 with a 35mm lens.

Two field workers in hi-vis and hard hats standing on a revegetated mine
slope, one holding a rugged tablet, the other pointing toward a contour
bank of young native seedlings. Natural, candid working posture — no eye
contact with camera, faces angled away or in profile.

Setting: rehabilitated Australian mine land — terraced ochre and rust
substrate softened by establishing vegetation, haul road visible far
below, big open sky.

Late afternoon light, long shadows, slight dust haze.

The mood is quiet competence: evidence being collected, progress being
measured.

No text. No logos on clothing. No UI chrome.

## IMAGE 13 — Winter Crop Aerial

3840x2160

Drone photograph, DJI Inspire 3, 60–80m altitude, oblique angle.

Australian winter cropping country in full green flush — deep emerald
canola and wheat paddocks divided by darker tree lines and red-brown
access tracks, a dam catching the sky.

Soft overcast-break morning light, mist lifting off the low ground.

Cooler, warier palette than the golden-hour set — this frame provides the
green season counterpoint in the same campaign grade: slightly
desaturated, medium contrast, rich earth tones in the tracks and soil.

No text. No logos. No UI chrome. No overlay on this one — pure
photography.

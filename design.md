## Shadows

- Very subtle.
- No heavy blur.
- Large soft shadows only.

---

## Motion

Motion library: `motion`

Use:

- fade
- stagger
- scale
- parallax
- number counters

Rules:

- No bouncing.
- No flashy animations.
- Animation duration: 0.4–0.8s
- Ease: easeOut
